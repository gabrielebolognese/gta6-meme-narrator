# How the narrator is funny

The narrator is a calm, deadpan documentary voice that understands nothing. It narrates exactly what happens on screen, but reads every
action wrongly, always with total confidence. The picture shows the truth; the voice gets it wrong; the gap is the joke.

## The moves

- **The wholesome misread.** Violence becomes kindness, chaos becomes a plan. An NPC punches the player: "This kind man saw a fly on her
  shoulder and decided to get rid of it." A car flips: "He wanted to see the sky one more time."
- **Names and backstories for strangers.** Every random NPC gets a plain name and a petty motive: "This is Brenda. Brenda saw nothing.
  Brenda is legally a lamp now." "Gerald has been training for this parking spot since 2019."
- **The disaster that worked.** "And that is how you win a race. You just leave it." "Flawless. No notes."
- **Callbacks.** A name or a phrase from an early line comes back at the end ("Steve wanted a nap too. It's contagious.").
- **Understatement on the peak, overstatement on nothing.** The explosion gets "Slight change of plans." A man standing still gets
  "Absolute cinema."
- **The last line lands the short:** a callback, a verdict ("Flawless show.") or a fake moral.

## The wrong name (optional, the strongest hook)

The hook line calls the streamer or player by a famous wrong name, as if the narrator mixed them up: "When Taylor Swift was playing GTA 6",
"Gordon Ramsay after one hour of GTA 6", "MrBeast trying GTA 6 for the first time". Keep the wrong name through the short (or never mention
it again); nobody corrects it. Pick famous, harmless names (singers, athletes, chefs, cartoon characters). Set `wrong_name` in edit.json so
the check makes sure the hook line says it. Use it on about half of the shorts, so it stays a surprise.

## The glitch rule

A glitch, a bug or broken physics always gets a "we waited 10 years for this" kind of line ("Ten years of waiting. Worth it.", "Rockstar
had a decade for this exact moment") and the `glitch` sound on it.

## Timing

- A line of N words takes about N/3 seconds. Leave a gap after each line.
- Leave the 2 or 3 best real reactions free: no line over the streamer's scream or the funniest game sound (the clip is turned down while
  the narrator talks).
- The first line (the hook line) is over the cold open; the next one starts right after the rewind, on the build-up.
- One joke per line. Short sentences. Plain spoken words; slang where it lands (cooked, no chill, aura, bro, lowkey).

## Arrows, freezes, memes, sounds

- An arrow points at what the line is about, at the moment the line says it ("Now she spots Linda" while the arrow says LINDA).
- A freeze goes on the face right before a disaster or the reaction right after it, with a zoom on that face.
- An inserted meme answers the line before it (the narrator says something absurd, cut to The Rock raising an eyebrow).
- Sounds land on the action, never on the first word of a line: a boom on the punch, a buzzer on a fail, the scratch before a twist, the
  air horn on a win, the sad trombone on a slow defeat.

## Never

- Never just describe ("He is driving. Now he crashes.").
- Never explain a joke or wink at it ("get it?").
- No slurs, nothing sexual about real people, no claim that a real person did something illegal outside the game, no hate, light swearing
  at most. A wrong name is always obviously a joke.
